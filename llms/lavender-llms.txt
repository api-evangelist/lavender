# Lavender

> Lavender is an AI email intelligence company for sales teams. Its Email Coach browser extension and
> Outlook add-in score and coach outbound sales email in real time using models trained on billions of
> sales emails; its Ora product is an AI sales agent that drafts email and pushes reasoned prospect
> research into Salesforce. Lavender ships as an end-user extension and a CRM integration — it publishes
> no public developer API, API reference, OpenAPI definition, or client SDKs.

## Products

- [Email Coach](https://www.lavender.ai/coach): Real-time scoring and coaching for sales email in Gmail, Outlook, and supported sales engagement tools.
- [Ora](https://www.ora.im): AI sales agent that drafts email and reasons through prospect research.
- [CRM Enrichment](https://www.lavender.ai/crm): Pushes reasoned research into Salesforce contact and account records. Available to Salesforce CRM customers.
- [Install](https://install.lavender.ai): Add the Email Coach to Chrome.
- [Dashboard](https://dashboard.lavender.ai/): Account sign-in.

## Developer surface

- No public API, developer portal, or API reference is published as of 2026-07-19.
- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, or gRPC definition is published.
- No first-party client SDKs or CLI are published on npm, PyPI, or other public registries.
- No webhook or event catalog is published.
- No `/.well-known/` discovery documents are served on `www.lavender.ai` or `api.lavender.ai`.
- Integration with Lavender is via the browser extension, the Outlook add-in, and the Salesforce integration.
